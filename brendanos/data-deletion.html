LifeOS (BrendanOS) Data Deletion

Last updated: July 2026

LifeOS is multi-user. You have the right to have your personal data erased.

How to Request Deletion

Email brendan@everlight-digital.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm in writing what was deleted and when — target turnaround 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account removes your user row and everything tied to it — captures, tasks, entities, daily logs, memory/embeddings, audit log, scripture, sermons, weekly reviews, login challenges, Telegram subscription, and API usage records. No orphaned data is left behind.

What's Outside Our Control

If you used the Telegram capture bot, your own message history on Telegram's side is separate from LifeOS and must be cleared within Telegram itself. If you connected X or Threads, posts already published to those platforms must be deleted there directly.
