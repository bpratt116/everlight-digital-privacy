The problem

Your watch or ring collects sleep, heart rate, and workout data all day, but most fitness apps hand it back as a wall of charts with no clear answer to the actual question: am I ready to push today, or should I back off? Regen pulls in your HealthKit data and turns it into one clear recovery read, so you can make that call in seconds instead of interpreting graphs.

What it does

One recovery score, not ten charts

Sleep, heart rate variability, and strain roll up into a single daily readiness read.

Built on your own data

Pulls straight from HealthKit — whatever your watch or ring already tracks — instead of asking you to log anything new.

Trends over time, not just today

See how recovery is trending across weeks, so you can catch overtraining before it turns into a setback.

A look inside

Who it's for

Anyone training regularly who wears an Apple Watch or similar device and wants a straight answer on recovery instead of raw data to decode themselves.
