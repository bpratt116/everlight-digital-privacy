The problem

Running a volunteer event usually means a spreadsheet, a group text, and a sign-up form that don't talk to each other — and someone finds out two people were double-booked for the same shift the morning of. EventFlow puts the event, the shifts, and the volunteers who are actually available in one place, so the person organizing isn't the only one holding the whole thing together in their head.

What it does

Build an event in minutes

Set up shifts, roles, and capacity for an event without wrestling a spreadsheet into shape first.

Volunteers sign up themselves

Share one link — volunteers pick their own shift and see it confirmed, no back-and-forth needed.

See gaps before the day of

A live view of which shifts are covered and which aren't, so you can go recruit the right person early instead of scrambling.

Who it's for

Volunteer coordinators, event organizers, and anyone running recurring events who's tired of chasing sign-ups across text threads and spreadsheets.
