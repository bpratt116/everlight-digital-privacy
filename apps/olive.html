A calm home for your family's week

The problem

Running a household means holding a hundred small things at once — who has practice, what's for dinner, what's running low, whose turn it is. It ends up scattered across texts, sticky notes, a shared calendar nobody checks, and one parent's memory. Olive pulls the moving parts of family life into one calm place the whole household can see, without turning home into another inbox to manage.

What it does

The week, at a glance

Tasks, routines, and the family calendar in one shared today view — everyone sees the same plan.

Meals and groceries, connected

Plan the week's meals and let the grocery list build itself, so the run to the store isn't a guessing game.

Shared without the nagging

Routines and chores that help the household run, without badges and streaks guilt-tripping anyone.

Who it's for

Families and busy households who want one calm, shared place for the week's plans — not another app that turns family life into a chore chart.
