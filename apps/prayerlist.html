Pilgrim: Prayer List

Remember the request. Make time to pray. Follow up with the person.
iOS · Separate from the Pilgrim Bible app
Get it on the App Store

The problem

Prayer requests arrive in texts, hallway conversations, and Sunday mornings — then slip away before you pray or check back in. Prayer List is a quiet place to capture what you heard, pray through it, and remember to follow up with the person. It is not a Bible reader and not the Pilgrim Bible & Prayer app.

What it does

Capture the request

Write down people and situations when you hear them, so the ask is still there when you sit down to pray.

Pray through the list

Work through active prayers at your own pace. Mark them answered when they are answered — answered prayers do not count against the free cap of 10 active prayers.

Follow up with the person

Keep enough context to check back in — not just to pray once and forget.

Plus when you need more

Plus unlocks a bigger list, named lists, People, the widget, and the year in review. Sharing is off.

On your device

Prayers stay on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync in your private iCloud database. Everlight does not run a server that receives prayer content, and there is no separate Prayer List account.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a dedicated Prayer List — separate from Bible reading — to remember requests, make time to pray, and follow up with the people they care about.
