The problem

A pastor can hold a few hundred names and stories in their head, but past that, care starts running on memory instead of on record — who's in the hospital, who hasn't been checked on in months, who asked for prayer three weeks ago and never got a follow-up. Most church management software is built for the office, not for the person actually doing the visiting. Shepherd is built around the pastor's actual week: people, prayer requests, and notes, kept in one place that's fast enough to use between visits, not just at a desk.

What it does

A living record of your people

Families, relationships, and care history in one profile per person, so context is there before you walk in the door.

Prayer requests that don't get lost

Track prayer requests from ask to answer, with categories and reminders instead of a stack of sticky notes.

Notes with real visibility control

Write a note as shared with the team, private to you, or confidential to clergy — the right people see it, and only them.

A prayer list your whole team can see

Requests move from ask to answer in one shared view, so nobody's praying for something that already got resolved last week.

Who it's for

Pastors, elders, and church staff who are responsible for caring well for a congregation and want to actually remember what's going on with people, not just manage a database.
