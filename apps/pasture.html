The problem

Herd records tend to live in a notebook in the truck, a spreadsheet on one computer, and someone's memory — which works fine until a tag number needs to be looked up in the field or a vet visit needs to be tied back to a specific animal's history. Pasture keeps every animal's weight, health, and breeding records in one place you can pull up from a phone standing in the pasture, not just from a desk back at the house.

What it does

Every animal, one record

Tag number, weight history, and health notes for each animal, kept together instead of scattered across notebooks.

Works from the field

Pull up or log an animal's record on your phone while you're standing right there with it, no need to walk back to a computer.

Herd-level view, not just per-animal

See weight trends and health patterns across the whole herd, so problems show up before they're a crisis.

Who it's for

Cattle and herd owners who want their records off paper and off a single spreadsheet, and accessible from wherever the animals actually are.
