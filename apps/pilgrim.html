Pilgrim Bible

Get it on the App Store

The problem

Most Bible apps turn reading into a streak to protect — miss a day and the app makes you feel it. Pilgrim starts from a different premise: reading Scripture is something you come back to because it matters, not because a counter is watching. It's a quiet place to read a passage and mark it up, with nothing tracking how "consistent" you've been.

What it does

Read without the scoreboard

Full Bible text with clean typography and no streak counter, no red badge nagging you back in.

Mark verses that matter

Highlight, note, and bookmark passages as you read, so the verses that hit stay easy to find later.

Commentary beside the verse

Matthew Henry, John Gill, and Vincent's Word Studies on key books, right next to the text and fully offline.

Plans for every season

Bible in a Year, Psalms in 30 days, the Gospels, and more. Missed days stay open, and you can invite a friend to read a plan with you.

A look inside

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a daily Bible habit that doesn't run on guilt — from someone starting out to a longtime reader tired of gamified faith apps.
