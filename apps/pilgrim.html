The problem

Most Bible apps turn reading into a streak to protect — miss a day and the app makes you feel it. Pilgrim starts from a different premise: reading Scripture and praying are things you come back to because they matter, not because a counter is watching. It's a quiet place to read a passage, mark it up, and pray, with nothing tracking how "consistent" you've been.

What it does

Read without the scoreboard

Full Bible text with clean typography and no streak counter, no red badge nagging you back in.

Mark verses that matter

Highlight, note, and bookmark passages as you read, so the verses that hit stay easy to find later.

A real prayer list

Keep a running list of who and what you're praying for, and check in on it whenever you actually want to — not on a schedule the app sets.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a daily Bible and prayer habit that doesn't run on guilt — from someone starting out to a longtime reader tired of gamified faith apps.
