About Persue Prayer

Persue Prayer is a prayer-focused app with ACTS / PRAY / Simple methods, prayer lists, answered prayers, streaks, sermon notes, memory verses, accountability check-ins, and mortification tracking.

Common Questions

How do I create a prayer list?

Tap Prayers → Lists → New List. Add prayers, set a schedule, then start a session.

My streak reset even though I prayed yesterday.

Streaks are based on your local time zone. If you traveled across time zones it can affect the count. Email us with your dates and we can verify.

Can I lock the app with Face ID?

Yes. Settings → Privacy → Biometric Lock. Face ID / Touch ID matching is on-device only.

Are my mortification entries private?

Yes. They are stored in your private iCloud (CloudKit) container and excluded from Spotlight indexing. We do not have access. See privacy.

How do I sync between devices?

Persue Prayer currently syncs via your private iCloud (with migration to Firebase in progress). Same iCloud account on each device + iCloud Drive on.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Persue Prayer → Cancel.

How do I delete my account or data?

Settings → Account → Delete Account. Removes Firebase identity; CloudKit data is controlled by you.
