Persue Prayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Everlight Digital ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Persue Prayer app ("Persue Prayer").

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email and authentication identifier when you sign in.
  • Your content: Prayer lists, prayer entries, answered prayers, schedules, streaks, journal entries, sermon notes, mortification (sin tracking) entries, accountability check-ins, memory verses, and reading-plan progress. This sensitive personal-reflection content is stored in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) container, with migration to Google Firebase in progress.
  • Biometric authentication: If you enable Face ID or Touch ID app lock, biometric matching happens entirely on-device via Apple's Local Authentication framework. We never receive biometric data.
  • Device data: Device model, iOS version, app version, and crash logs.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and identify your subscription status
  • To sync your prayers and journal across your devices
  • To send the prayer reminders and notifications you have enabled
  • To deliver in-app purchases and manage your subscription
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app

How We Share Information

  • Apple (CloudKit, Sign in with Apple, App Store, push notifications) — sync, auth, delivery
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore — migration in progress) — sign-in and sync
  • RevenueCat — subscription management

We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising trackers. Sensitive content (mortification entries, accountability check-ins) is excluded from Spotlight indexing.

Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can export and delete your data from Settings > Data. Account deletion removes your Firebase identity and removes Firestore data within 30 days; CloudKit data is controlled by you in your iCloud account.

Your Rights

EU/UK users have GDPR rights; California users have CCPA rights. Contact bpratt116@gmail.com to exercise them.

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Authentication tokens are stored in iOS Keychain. Biometric matching is on-device only.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app.
