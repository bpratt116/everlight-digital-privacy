About Persue Bible

Persue Bible is a Bible app with multiple translations, curated reading plans, marked verses, journal, sermon notes, memorization, and the Theographic knowledge graph (people, places, atlas).

Common Questions

What's free vs. paid?

  • Free: all translations, one active reading plan, three journal entries
  • Pro ($3.99/mo or $39.99/yr): unlimited reading plans and journal entries, plus extras described on the upgrade screen

How do I start a reading plan?

Tap Plans → pick a plan → Start. Daily readings appear on Home. Tap a day's reading to open the reader at that passage.

How do I sync between my devices?

Persue Bible uses your private iCloud (CloudKit) for content sync. Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account and that iCloud Drive is on. Sign-in identity is via Firebase (Apple, Google, or email).

How do I switch translations?

In the reader, tap the translation name in the top bar to open the picker.

What is the atlas / people / places browser?

Theographic data: tap a person or place in the reader to open its entry. Or browse from People / Places / Atlas.

How do I cancel Pro?

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Persue Bible → Cancel.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete Account. Removes your Firebase identity. Your reading data lives in your private iCloud and is controlled by you.
