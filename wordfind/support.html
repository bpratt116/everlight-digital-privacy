About WordFind

WordFind is a clean daily word-search game with categories, difficulty levels, hints, and stats. iPhone and iPad.

Common Questions

How does the daily puzzle work?

A new puzzle drops every day. Same puzzle for everyone playing on the same date. Streak resets if you miss a day.

My streak vanished after updating the app.

Stats are stored locally. If they vanished after an update, email us with your last-known streak count and the update version. We'll investigate.

I found a word that's offensive / inappropriate.

Email us the exact word and the category it appeared in. We screen word lists but occasionally a borderline word slips through; we'll remove it.

Word selection is jumpy on diagonals.

Make sure your finger stays on the screen through the entire word. If selection still misbehaves, send a screen recording so we can reproduce.

How do I reset stats?

Stats → Reset. This is permanent.
