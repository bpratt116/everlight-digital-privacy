WordFind Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Everlight Digital ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the WordFind app ("WordFind").

Information We Collect

  • Game data: Puzzle progress, daily-puzzle completions, streaks, and stats stored locally on your device.
  • Settings: Theme and preferences stored locally.
  • Device data: Device model, iOS version, app version, and crash logs.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): If WordFind ships with ads enabled and you grant App Tracking Transparency permission, Google AdMob may use IDFA to deliver personalized ads. If you decline, ads are non-personalized. If ads are not yet enabled in this build, this section does not apply.

How We Use Information

  • To save your puzzle progress and stats locally
  • To deliver ads (free tier, when enabled)
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the app

How We Share Information

  • Google AdMob (when enabled) — advertising. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store)

WordFind has no backend account system. We do not sell your data. We do not collect personally identifying information.

Children

WordFind is intended for general audiences. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your Rights

You can revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. EU/UK users have GDPR rights; California users have CCPA rights. Contact bpratt116@gmail.com.

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. App data is sandboxed per Apple platform standards.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app.
