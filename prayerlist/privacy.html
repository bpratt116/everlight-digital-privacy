Pilgrim: Prayer List Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy describes how Everlight Digital handles information in Pilgrim: Prayer List on iPhone and iPad.

Prayer requests, gratitude notes, list names, and optional person names are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they also sync in your private iCloud database. We cannot read that database. Prayer logs are religious data: nothing is saved until you agree on first launch.

Purchases of Plus (monthly, yearly, or lifetime) are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card. Restore uses your Apple ID.

If you pick a person from Contacts, the app stores a name and optionally a Contacts identifier on your device only. Export omits that identifier.

There is no Everlight Digital account, no advertising, and no analytics SDK in version 1. The app may send local notifications you turn on.

You can export JSON from Settings, restore a recently deleted prayer for 30 days, or delete all prayers. Removing the app deletes local data. iCloud copies follow your Apple Account.

This app is not directed to children under 13.
