Chrona Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Everlight Digital ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Chrona calendar and task app ("Chrona") on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Chrona has no accounts, no login, and no server of ours. Your calendar, events, tasks, and attachments live on your device and, if you enable it, sync only through your own private Apple iCloud account. We never see your content.

Information We Collect

  • Your calendar and task content (stored on-device): Events, tasks, notes, checklists, tags, projects, and file attachments you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData.
  • iCloud sync (optional, your private account): If you are signed into iCloud, this content syncs between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit private database. It is stored in your personal iCloud account, not on any Everlight server, and is not visible to us.
  • Approximate location (only for weather): If you grant location permission, Chrona uses your approximate location to fetch a local 10-day forecast from Apple WeatherKit. Location is used only to request weather and is not stored by us or transmitted to any Everlight server.
  • Purchases: Chrona Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Device / diagnostic data: If you choose to share it with Apple, standard crash logs and diagnostics may be provided to us in aggregated, non-identifying form through App Store Connect.

How We Use Information

  • To store and display your calendar, events, and tasks
  • To sync your content across your own devices via your private iCloud account
  • To show a local weather forecast in your calendar
  • To deliver the reminders and notifications you have enabled

How We Share Information

  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — stores your synced content in your own private iCloud account; controlled by you in iOS/macOS Settings
  • Apple WeatherKit — receives an approximate location to return a forecast, per Apple's weather data terms
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — processes purchases and subscriptions

We do not sell your data, Chrona contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and your calendar and task content never leaves your device except through your own private iCloud account.

Children

Chrona is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your Rights

You can revoke location access at any time in iOS/macOS Settings > Privacy > Location Services > Chrona. You can turn off iCloud sync in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data on that device. EU/UK users have GDPR rights; California users have CCPA rights. Contact brendan@everlight-digital.com.

Security

App data is sandboxed and encrypted per Apple platform standards. iCloud content is protected by your Apple ID and Apple's CloudKit security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app.
