Pilgrim Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Everlight Digital ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Pilgrim: Bible & Prayer iOS app.

What We Collect

Nothing on our servers. Pilgrim has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server-side database that Everlight Digital operates.

What Stays on Your Device + iCloud

  • Your highlights, notes, sermon notes, prayer requests, reading-plan progress, and memory-card progress are stored locally on your device and synced through your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database. Only you can read them.
  • Private Circles. If you create or join a Circle (an invite-only group of up to 12 members), posts in that Circle are stored in an Apple CloudKit shared zone that only members of that Circle can read. Everlight Digital cannot read this content.
  • Display name. The name you set in Pilgrim's profile is visible only to other members of any Circles you join. It is not shared with Everlight Digital.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple CloudKit — sync and shared zones. Subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • api.esv.org — Bible passage requests are sent through our Cloudflare Worker proxy so your IP address is not exposed to Crossway. The proxy caches responses to minimize traffic. Subject to Crossway's privacy policy.
  • API.Bible (American Bible Society) — Bible passage requests for CSB / NIV / NLT translations, proxied through our Cloudflare Worker. Subject to API.Bible's terms.
  • Cloudflare — runs the Bible-API proxy Worker. Subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What We Do Not Do

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No In-App Purchase, no subscription, no payment processing in v1
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of any kind

Children

Pilgrim is designed for adult Christians and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Choices

  • Delete the app at any time. Local data is removed by iOS. iCloud data can be deleted from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Leave a Circle from inside Pilgrim. All of that Circle's local cache is removed from your device.
